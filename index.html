Clio is a private AI assistant that Alex Jukes runs for his own use. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

With the owner's permission, Clio reads his Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive. It uses them to prepare daily briefings, file routine email into labels, and draft replies for him to review. It never sends email on its own.
